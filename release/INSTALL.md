# SeaClient Installation Guide

### Requirements
- **Minecraft**: 26.3
- **Mod Loader**: Fabric Loader (>= 0.18.0)
- **Fabric API**: 0.165.2+26.3 (or compatible for 26.3)
- **Java**: Java 25 or higher

---

### Quick Installation

1. **Install Fabric Loader**:
   - Download the official Fabric Installer from [fabricmc.net](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/).
   - Run the installer and choose **Minecraft 26.3** with Fabric Loader **0.20.1** (or latest).

2. **Add Fabric API**:
   - Download the compatible Fabric API `.jar` for Minecraft 26.3.
   - Place it into your `.minecraft/mods` directory.

3. **Install SeaClient**:
   - Copy `SeaClient-1.0.0.jar` into your `.minecraft/mods` directory:
     - **Windows**: `%appdata%\.minecraft\mods\`
     - **macOS**: `~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/mods/`
     - **Linux**: `~/.minecraft/mods/`

4. **Launch the Game**:
   - Open your Minecraft Launcher, select the **Fabric-Loader-26.3** profile, and launch!

---

### In-Game Controls & Features

- **Optimizer Menu**: Press `Right-Shift` to open the custom SeaClient Optimization & Bedwars Settings UI.
- **Title Screen Controls**:
  - Interactive 3D Player Skin: Click and drag anywhere on your skin to rotate.
  - Quick Continue Card: One-click re-entry into your last played singleplayer world or multiplayer server.
  - Live Media Player: Embedded Windows System Media widget automatically mirrors playing tracks.
- **Hypixel Bedwars & PvP Overlays**:
  - CPS counter (LMB & RMB).
  - Reach distance and combo tracking.
  - Generator timers (Diamond / Emerald tier spawns).
  - Live inventory tracking for wool blocks and resources.
